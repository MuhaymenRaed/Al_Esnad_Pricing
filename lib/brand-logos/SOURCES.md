# Brand logos — sources and licences

The bundled logos identify the mobile phone and tablet brands the company sells
(in the app and in PDF reports). Every logo is a trademark of its owner;
inclusion does not imply any endorsement.

Files: `public/brand-logos/<key>.svg` (web), `lib/brand-logos/index.ts`
(name → logo lookup) and `lib/brand-logos/paths.ts` (vector shapes for PDFs).
They were generated from the sources below (bounding boxes measured in a
browser; colours are each brand's official colour). A brand added later without
a bundled logo shows a text wordmark — an administrator can also set a logo URL
per brand.

| Logos | Source | Licence |
|---|---|---|
| Apple, Samsung, Xiaomi, Honor, Huawei, OPPO, vivo, OnePlus, Google, Motorola, Nokia, Lenovo | [Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org) 16.36.0 | CC0 1.0 |
| TECNO, ZTE | [theSVG](https://github.com/glincker/thesvg) (colour set) | MIT (notice below) |
| realme, Infinix, itel, Nothing | Wikimedia Commons: *Realme logo.svg*, *Infinix logo.svg*, *Itel Mobile logo 2023.svg*, *Nothing.svg* | Public domain (text logos) |
